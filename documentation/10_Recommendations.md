# Business Recommendations

Recommendations are based on the analytical findings identified in the E-Commerce Business Intelligence Dashboard.

## 1. Product Category Optimization

**Problem:** Revenue contribution varies across product categories.

**Evidence:** Categories such as Health & Beauty and Watches & Gifts are among the top revenue-generating categories.

**Recommendation:** Focus on high-performing categories, maintain sufficient inventory, and improve marketing strategies for low-performing categories.

**Expected Impact:** Increased sales, better inventory management, and improved revenue.

**Priority:** High

## 2. Regional Market Expansion

**Problem:** Revenue is concentrated in a few high-performing states.

**Evidence:** São Paulo (SP) contributes the highest revenue among the states displayed in the dashboard.

**Recommendation:** Strengthen operations in high-performing regions and introduce targeted marketing campaigns in other states.

**Expected Impact:** Increased regional sales and reduced dependency on a single market.

**Priority:** High

## 3. Order Delivery Improvement

**Problem:** Non-delivered and delayed orders can negatively affect customer experience.

**Evidence:** Approximately 97% of orders have a Delivered status in the dashboard.

**Recommendation:** Monitor cancelled and delayed orders, identify delivery issues, and improve logistics coordination.

**Expected Impact:** Better delivery performance and improved customer satisfaction.

**Priority:** High

## 4. Customer Satisfaction Improvement

**Problem:** Negative customer reviews indicate potential product or service quality issues.

**Evidence:** The dashboard shows different review-score distributions, including low-rated reviews.

**Recommendation:** Analyze negative reviews, identify recurring complaints, and improve product quality and customer support.

**Expected Impact:** Higher customer satisfaction and improved customer trust.

**Priority:** High

## 5. Payment Experience Optimization

**Problem:** Customers use different payment methods, requiring a reliable checkout experience.

**Evidence:** Credit cards represent the largest payment category in the dashboard.

**Recommendation:** Maintain a smooth credit-card checkout process and continue supporting multiple payment options.

**Expected Impact:** Better payment experience and potentially fewer failed transactions.

**Priority:** Medium

## 6. Seller Performance Management

**Problem:** Seller performance can vary in terms of revenue, customer ratings, and delivery reliability.

**Evidence:** The dashboard identifies the top 10 sellers based on revenue.

**Recommendation:** Monitor seller performance regularly and strengthen relationships with reliable, high-performing sellers.

**Expected Impact:** Improved seller reliability and more consistent customer experience.

**Priority:** Medium

## 7. Monthly Revenue Monitoring

**Problem:** Changes in monthly revenue can make business performance difficult to predict.

**Evidence:** The monthly revenue comparison chart shows variations across months and years.

**Recommendation:** Monitor monthly revenue trends, investigate unusual declines, and plan marketing campaigns based on validated seasonal patterns.

**Expected Impact:** Better sales planning and improved revenue forecasting.

**Priority:** Medium

## Conclusion

These recommendations provide actionable business strategies based on the dashboard findings. Implementing and monitoring these actions can help improve revenue performance, customer satisfaction, delivery operations, and overall business efficiency.
