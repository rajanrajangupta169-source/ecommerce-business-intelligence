# 1. Python Exploratory Data Analysis

## Objective

The objective of Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) was to examine the
cleaned Olist e-commerce datasets and identify meaningful business
patterns, trends, relationships, and performance indicators.

The analysis was performed using Python, Pandas, NumPy, and Matplotlib.

---

## 2 Dataset Overview

A structured overview of the cleaned datasets was performed.

The analysis included dataset dimensions, column information, data types,
missing values, duplicate records, and unique-value counts.

This provided an understanding of the structure and quality of the
datasets before detailed business analysis.

---

## 3 Descriptive Statistics

Descriptive statistics were calculated for numerical variables.

The analysis included count, mean, median, standard deviation, minimum,
maximum, and quartiles.

Categorical variables were also examined using unique-value counts and
frequency distributions.

---

## 4 Order Analysis

Order status distribution was analyzed to understand the order lifecycle.

Monthly order trends were also examined to identify changes in order
volume over time.

---

## 5 Sales & Revenue Analysis

Sales and revenue analysis was performed using order-item transaction
data.

The analysis included:

- Total revenue
- Average Order Value (AOV)
- Monthly revenue
- Revenue trends

---

## 6 Product & Category Analysis

Product and category performance was analyzed using product and order-item
data.

The analysis identified top product categories and products based on
revenue and sales volume.

---

## 7 Customer Analysis

Customer analysis examined customer distribution across states and
customer-level spending.

This helped understand customer concentration and purchasing behavior.

---

## 8 Seller Analysis

Seller performance was analyzed using revenue generated by sellers.

Top sellers were identified based on transaction revenue.

---

## 9 Payment Analysis

Payment analysis examined payment methods, payment values, and installment
patterns.

This provides insight into customer payment preferences.

---

## 10 Review & Rating Analysis

Review scores were analyzed to understand customer satisfaction patterns.

The analysis included review-score distribution and average review score.

---

## 11 Delivery Performance

Delivery performance was analyzed by calculating actual delivery duration
and comparing actual delivery dates with estimated delivery dates.

Orders were categorized as on-time or late based on the comparison.

---

## 12 Geographic Analysis

Geographic analysis examined customer and seller distribution across
states.

This helps identify important geographic markets and seller locations.

---

## 13 Time-Series Analysis

Order trends were analyzed over time using yearly and monthly
aggregations.

This helps identify changes in business activity over the available
period.

## 15 Business KPI Analysis

The final Business KPI analysis was performed to provide a consolidated
view of the overall e-commerce business performance.

The following key performance indicators were calculated from the cleaned
datasets.

| KPI | Value |
|---|---:|
| Total Orders | 99,441 |
| Total Customers | 96,096 |
| Total Products | 32,951 |
| Total Sellers | 3,095 |
| Total Revenue | 13,591,643.70 |
| Average Order Value | 137.75 |
| Average Review Score | 4.09 |

### KPI Interpretation

- **Total Orders:** The analysis identified 99,441 unique orders in the
  dataset.

- **Total Customers:** The dataset contains 96,096 unique customers based
  on the customer unique identifier.

- **Total Products:** The product catalog contains 32,951 unique products.

- **Total Sellers:** A total of 3,095 sellers are represented in the
  dataset.

- **Total Revenue:** The total transaction revenue calculated from the
  order-item price data is 13,591,643.70.

- **Average Order Value (AOV):** The calculated average order value is
  137.75.

- **Average Review Score:** The average customer review score is 4.09.

These KPIs provide a high-level summary of the business and will also
serve as important metrics for the final Power BI dashboard.
