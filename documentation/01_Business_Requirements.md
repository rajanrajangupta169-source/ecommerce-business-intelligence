# E-Commerce Business Intelligence Project

## 1. Business Background

The company operates an e-commerce marketplace where
customers purchase products from multiple sellers.

The company has transactional data covering customers,
orders, products, payments, reviews, sellers, and
geographic information.

Management wants to use this data to improve sales,
profitability, customer retention, product performance,
and operational efficiency.

---

## 2. Business Problem

Management currently lacks a centralized analytical
view of business performance.

Key challenges include:

- Limited visibility into revenue and profit trends
- Difficulty identifying profitable and unprofitable products
- Limited understanding of customer purchasing behavior
- Difficulty identifying high-value customers
- Unclear impact of discounts on profitability
- Limited visibility into order delivery and review performance

---

## 3. Business Objective

The objective of this project is to transform raw
e-commerce transactional data into actionable business
insights that can help management:

- Increase revenue
- Improve profitability
- Understand customer behavior
- Improve product performance
- Reduce operational issues
- Support data-driven decision making

---

## 4. Key Business Questions

### Sales

1. What is the overall revenue trend?
2. Which categories generate the highest revenue?
3. Which regions generate the most sales?

### Profitability

4. Which products generate the highest profit?
5. Which products have high sales but low profitability?
6. How does discounting affect profitability?

### Customers

7. Who are the highest-value customers?
8. What percentage of customers make repeat purchases?
9. Which customer segments contribute most to revenue?

### Products

10. Which products are best-selling?
11. Which products are underperforming?
12. Which categories have the strongest margins?

### Operations

13. What is the order delivery performance?
14. What is the return/review-related performance?
15. Which areas have operational issues?

---

## 5. Target Stakeholders

- Head of Sales
- Business Manager
- Marketing Team
- Operations Team
- Senior Management

---

## 6. Expected Deliverables

- Clean analytical datasets
- SQL analysis

---

## 7. Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful if stakeholders
can use the final analytical solution to understand
business performance and identify actionable opportunities
for revenue growth and profitability improvement.
